## The shoe

- **Name**: Gully Number 001 – Baaz Kora White. Men UK 6–12, women's sizing on a separate listing. No half sizes ("Between sizes? Size up.").
- **Price on the page at time of research**: ₹4,290 ("Previously ₹5,490"). The long description on the same page says ₹4,999 — the page disagrees with itself, so the site links out instead of leaning on a number.
- **Silhouette**: low-top, flat cupsole court/skate shape, wide rounded toe, padded collar. Positioned as "a skate and dance shoe" — "made for dancers, skaters and folks who are always on the move."
- **Colour**: the brand describes Kora as "a clean, unbleached white". In the photography it is clearly not optical white: warm ecru canvas (sampled median ≈ `rgb(238,216,184)`), a tan trim (≈ `rgb(153,103,56)`), and an off-white sole. Against the white studio backdrop the shoe reads beige.
- **Materials / construction (features list)**:
  - "Baaz embroidery on back tab" (a tonal embroidered motif on the heel tab)
  - "Suede toe and vamp for durability"
  - "Canvas strip crafted Baaz trail" — the swept double stripe along the side
  - "Metal eyelets and circular laces"
  - "Bamboo lycra lining to avoid odour"
  - "Memory Foam Cushioning | Natural Rubber Soles | Wide Toe | Terry Towel Inner Lining"
  - "hand-lasted… on a wider Indian foot form… wider across the ball, roomier at the toe"
- **Things visible in the photos that the copy underlines**:
  - Panel edges are left raw and frayed, outlined in tan. ("Raw edges, textured panels, and real wear energy, inspired by the streets.")
  - "The signature side trail runs the length of the shoe. A design detail for people who know what to look for."
  - The heel counters are printed across the pair: left shoe **गली**, right shoe **LABS**. You need both shoes to read the name.
  - Ships with two lace sets, cream and tan: "One shoe, two looks. Wear the mood you're in. Tap to change the laces."
  - Tongue label reads गलीLABS over a bird-head mark.
- **Discrepancy noted**: the long description calls the upper "top-grade softie leather"; the features list, page headline ("A daily-wear canvas sneaker") and every photo show canvas with suede-textured trim. The site follows the photos and the features list (canvas).

## The words (my reading, not brand copy)

- **Kora (कोरा)**: Hindi/Urdu adjective — unbleached (*kora kapda*, loom-state cloth), blank (*kora kaagaz*, a blank page), untouched/new.
- **Baaz (बाज़)**: falcon / hawk.
- So the name literally says *falcon, on a blank page* — and the shoe is exactly that: unbleached cloth with one line drawn across it.

## The brand

- Launched August 2023 from Delhi: "a household sneaker brand from Delhi that is truly and unapologetically Indian… shaped by local streets, crafts, and culture, not borrowed from the West."
- First workshop April 2024: 1,000 sq ft, Noida Sector-10, six karigars. Since January 2025: 10,000 sq ft in Noida Sector-4, "over 50 karigars".
- "Behind every pair you love, is a pair of hands with decades of expertise. Our karigars are the backbone of everything we're building."
- Founders Arjun Singh and Animesh Mishra. "Made by hand, powered by belief, and unapologetically Indian."
- Voice: short, direct, a little cocky ("because every main character needs a signature"), Hindi words used without translation. Packaging is cobalt blue with an orange interior.

## Concept that fell out of this

**"Not white."** A campaign page usually starts on a white page. This shoe's whole point is that it isn't white — it is what cloth looks like before bleach. So the page opens on bleached screen-white and the scroll pulls the bleach out, thread by thread, until the page itself is kora. Because the page is now the exact colour of the canvas, the shoe's body disappears into it and only the tan line is left: the Baaz trail, drawn on a blank page. Then the cloth takes shape around the line.
